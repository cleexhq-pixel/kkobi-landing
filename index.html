FAQ

자주 묻는 질문

Is Kkobi good for complete beginners learning Korean?
Yes! Kkobi is designed for all levels. If you're a complete beginner, we start with the most essential Korean phrases and build from there. You'll be speaking real Korean sentences on your very first day.
How is Kkobi different from Duolingo or other Korean apps?
Most Korean apps focus on reading and vocabulary drills. Kkobi is built specifically for speaking practice. You speak Korean out loud, our AI listens, and gives you instant pronunciation feedback — the closest thing to a Korean conversation partner available 24/7.
Can I use Kkobi to prepare for TOPIK?
Kkobi focuses on spoken Korean fluency, which is a great complement to TOPIK preparation. Strong speaking ability helps with listening comprehension and overall Korean language confidence.
What languages are supported besides Korean?
Kkobi currently provides hint translations in English and Indonesian, with more languages coming soon. The Korean conversation practice works for learners from any native language background.
